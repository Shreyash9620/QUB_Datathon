# Evidence & sources — PRAVAAH model parameters

All external assumptions in `pravaah_model.py`, with citations. Figures are national (India)
applied to Gujarat — stated explicitly and sensitivity-tested where they affect the headline.

## A2 · Employment intensity — 22 jobs per ₹1 crore (range 15–35)
- **WTTC 2024 (India):** Travel & Tourism contributed **₹21 trillion** to GDP and supported an
  all-time-high **46.5 million jobs** (9.1% of national employment).
  → 46.5M ÷ ₹21,00,000 cr = **22.1 jobs per ₹crore** of tourism economic activity (central estimate).
- **India Tourism Satellite Account / MoT:** tourism = **13.34% of total employment** (direct 5.82%)
  ⇒ a ~**2.3× direct-to-total** employment multiplier (indirect + induced).
- Applied conservatively (≈1:1 capital-to-annual-activity); reported as a **range 15–35 jobs/cr**
  (≈98,000–228,000 jobs) rather than a single false-precision figure.
- Sources: WTTC India Economic Impact 2024 (wttc.org); Tourism Satellite Account for India,
  Ministry of Tourism (tourism.gov.in); Business Economics / Drishti IAS summaries.

## A1 · Yield per visit — relative spend tiers (pilgrimage ₹800 → eco-premium ₹6,000)
- **WTTC 2024:** domestic travel spending = **₹15.5 trillion** (22% above 2019).
- Tiers are *relative* in-destination spend intensity by tourism type — pilgrimage day-trips spend
  far less than premium eco/Rann or heritage stays — anchored to an ~₹2,000 average domestic
  in-destination spend. This is the "footfall ≠ value" correction; treated as a sensitivity lever.

## A5 · Eco-sensitive district flags — notified Gujarat ESZs
- **Six approved Eco-Sensitive Zones in Gujarat** (MoEF): Marine National Park & Sanctuary
  (Gulf of Kachchh), Girnar WS, Narayan Sarovar WS, Purna WS, Vansda NP, Thol WS.
- **Gir National Park ESZ:** final draft notification **18 Sept 2024** (2.78–9.50 km buffer,
  ~2,061 sq km).
- Maps to flagged districts: Gir Somnath & Junagadh (Gir / Girnar), Kachchh (Marine NP / Narayan
  Sarovar), Devbhoomi Dwarka & Porbandar (coastal / marine), Dang (Purna / Vansda).
- Sources: PCCF & HoFF Gujarat (forests.gujarat.gov.in/esz-proposal-status.htm); MoEF Rajya Sabha
  reply (2016); Centre's Gir ESZ draft notification (Sept 2024).
